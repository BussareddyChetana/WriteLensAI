Features

A complete analysis suite, not just a label.

Everything below runs on every single analysis — no separate paid tier for the parts that actually explain the result.

Detection engine

How the classifier reads your text

Hybrid TF-IDF + Stylometric Model

Word and bigram frequency features are combined with sentence-length burstiness, vocabulary richness, punctuation density, and transition-word usage, so the model isn't relying on vocabulary alone.

Calibrated Confidence

Displayed confidence is deliberately capped — no analysis claims near-certainty. Predictions under a 15-point margin are reported as "Uncertain" rather than forced to a side.

Sentence-Level Inspection

Every sentence is evaluated individually and color-highlighted by risk level, with a specific, human-readable reason for each flag.

Estimated AI Source

An experimental signature match estimates likelihood across ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Llama, and Mistral — always shown with a clear disclaimer that this is a pattern estimate, not identification.

Insights & reporting

Understand the result, keep a record of it

Writing Statistics & Charts

Visual charts showing sentence-length burstiness, vocabulary richness, and type-token ratios for the exact text you submitted.

Downloadable PDF Reports

Export a publication-ready PDF audit report, formatted with ReportLab, including executive summary tables and charts.

History Management

Securely store, search, review, and delete every past analysis from your personal dashboard whenever you need to revisit a result.

Multi-Format Document Parsing

Upload raw text (.txt), PDF documents (.pdf), or Word files (.docx) with automatic text extraction — no manual copy-pasting required.

Private by Default

Analyses are tied to your account and visible only to you and admins responsible for platform integrity — never shared or sold.

Instant Results

Feature extraction and inference run in real time — no queueing, no waiting for a batch job to finish.
