About WriteLens AI

An instrument, not an oracle.

WriteLens AI was built to answer a narrower, more honest question than "is this AI?" — it measures how strongly a piece of writing exhibits statistical patterns associated with language-model output, and shows you exactly why.

Our approach

Four questions, not one label

Most detectors return a single word: "AI" or "Human." We think that's the least useful part of the answer. Every analysis on WriteLens AI is built to address four separate questions, each with its own evidence:

  • Prediction — is this human-written or AI-generated, based on the balance of evidence?
  • Confidence — how far the evidence sits from a coin-flip, capped so we never overstate certainty.
  • Why — which specific sentence structures, burstiness levels, and transition markers drove the result.
  • Estimated source — an experimental, clearly-labeled guess at which model family the writing resembles.
The machine learning engine

A hybrid feature set — TF-IDF word/bigram frequencies combined with 10+ stylometric measurements (sentence-length burstiness, vocabulary richness, transition-word density) — feeds three candidate classifiers (Logistic Regression, Linear SVM, Random Forest). The strongest performer on held-out data is selected automatically at training time.

Being upfront

What this tool can and can't tell you

What it's good for
  • Flagging text with strong, characteristic AI-writing patterns for a closer look
  • Explaining why a passage reads as synthetic — specific, checkable reasons
  • Giving a calibrated sense of uncertainty instead of a false binary
What it isn't
  • Proof of academic dishonesty or authorship fraud on its own
  • Immune to heavily edited or human-polished AI text, or unusual human styles
  • A source of guaranteed 99%+ certainty — we intentionally cap displayed confidence to reflect real-world limits

We'd rather under-claim than over-claim. If you're making a high-stakes decision based on an analysis, treat it as one input among several — not a verdict.
